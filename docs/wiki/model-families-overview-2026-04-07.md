# Model Families Overview

Date: 2026-04-07
Type: Dated research report

## Why this report matters

A serious AI wiki needs more than product names. It needs a map of the model families shaping the ecosystem.

This report focuses on the broad families that matter most to builders because they affect:

- capability
- deployment options
- licensing
- cost
- self-hosting strategy
- multimodal potential

## The main families to keep tracking

### OpenAI models

Why they matter:

- strong frontier performance
- deeply tied to the Codex and agent-product ecosystem
- strong relevance for coding, tool use, and production integrations

What to watch:

- coding-oriented model variants
- multimodal tooling support
- eval-driven product positioning

### Claude models

Why they matter:

- strong reasoning and coding workflows
- major importance in agentic terminal and product workflows
- strong trust/safety positioning

What to watch:

- how model tiers map to agent products
- reasoning vs speed tradeoffs
- tooling and workflow integration depth

### Gemini and Gemma

Why they matter:

- Gemini matters on the closed frontier side
- Gemma matters on the open side
- Google’s ecosystem matters for multimodal and developer tooling

Official Gemma docs currently emphasize:

- text and image input
- 140+ language support in Gemma 3
- multiple specialized variants such as CodeGemma, PaliGemma, ShieldGemma, and EmbeddingGemma

### Llama

Why it matters:

- one of the most strategically important open model families
- major ecosystem influence for self-hosting and downstream tooling

What to watch:

- context window advances
- multimodal capabilities
- license implications

### Qwen

Why it matters:

- strong open-weight multilingual and coding story
- increasingly important in agentic and open deployment stacks

What to watch:

- coding-focused variants
- multilingual and tool-use performance
- deployment support across inference stacks

### DeepSeek

Why it matters:

- major impact on open-weight reasoning and coding expectations
- important for cost/performance discussions

What to watch:

- reasoning variants
- tool-use integration
- licensing and deployment tradeoffs

### Mistral

Why it matters:

- strong open and commercial model lineup
- meaningful presence across cloud deployment platforms
- notable support for coding, OCR, moderation, and multimodal capabilities

Official Mistral docs currently highlight:

- Mistral Large 3
- Mistral Medium 3.1
- Mistral Small 4
- Devstral 2 for software engineering tasks
- OCR and moderation products

### Phi

Why it matters:

- small-model strategy matters for edge, efficiency, and constrained deployments

What to watch:

- reasoning performance per size
- practical deployment story

## What a builder should actually care about

When comparing model families, the important questions are:

- can it do the task reliably
- what inputs and outputs does it support
- what does it cost
- can it be self-hosted
- what are the license constraints
- how well does it fit an agent runtime

The wrong question is simply “which model is best.”
